## v2.0.0
### New menu
- Rebuilt from scratch. Every category is its own window with a coloured title bar and a plain list of options underneath. Drawn with simple shapes and one sans font, no game textures.
- Drag windows by the title bar, collapse them with the bar on the left of the title, scroll long ones. Position and collapsed state are remembered.
- Desktop gets the large, dense layout so many windows fit on screen at once. Phones get a smaller menu with taller rows.
- A floating black and white ball shows and hides everything. Drag it anywhere. On desktop **Tab + W** does the same.
- Tap an option to read what it does at the bottom of the screen.
- Seven themes (Violet, Midnight, Mono, Paper, Ocean, Forest, Rose). Menu size, ball size and layout reset are in the Theme window.

### Macro
- New **Editor** tab: select inputs, move them by frames, delete, duplicate, flip press and release, swap player, trim, shift the whole macro, add clicks, strip stored positions. Every edit can be undone.
- Controls for the level are now in the Macro tab: Pause, Resume, Restart, Step and Checkpoint + / -. This replaces the loose buttons that used to sit on top of the level screen and block the game's own pause and checkpoint buttons.
- The recording and playback labels in the level are gone.
- Macros are named automatically from the level, and the list shows how many inputs each one has.

### Platforms
- Added macOS and iOS. Windows, Android 64-bit and Android 32-bit stay supported.
- Targets Geode 6.0.0 and Geometry Dash 2.2091.
- Click Precision and Screen Record are Android only.

### Removed
- Quick Restart button (use Restart in the Macro tab), the in-level Step button, the Find tab, and the menu colour, accent and font settings.
