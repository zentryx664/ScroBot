# ScroBot

Frame-perfect macro recorder and player.

- **Record** your run, **Save** it, **Load** it from the list, and **Play** it back.
- Inputs are tied to the physics step, so playback has no delay and does not depend on FPS.
- Player position is corrected on every input, so replays stay smooth and in sync.
- Works with practice-mode checkpoints.

Open the pause menu and tap **Scro**.
